Questions

The things owners ask us.

Does the customer need to download an app?

No. They tap the card with their phone or scan the QR code, and your Google review page opens straight away in their browser. Nothing to install.

Will it work on every phone?

Yes. Modern iPhones and Android phones tap using NFC. Any phone with a camera can use the printed QR code instead, so nobody's left out.

Is there a monthly fee?

No subscriptions and no software to log into. You own the card outright and it keeps working.

How many should I get?

Most owners start with two: one at the counter or till, and one where customers wait or sit. More touchpoints means more taps. You can always add more later.

How do I set it up?

You don't. We link each card to your exact Google review page before it reaches you. Take it out of the packaging, stand it up, and it's working.
